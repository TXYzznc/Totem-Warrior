## Tuning targets

- Reduce the old sub-0.2s weapon loop into a readable 0.5s to 1.0s attack
  envelope.
- Keep light enemies killable quickly while preventing ranged basic attacks
  from dominating every close-range weapon.
- Slow Smart AI pressure while preserving the confirmed five personalities.
- Lower late shrink-zone and Boss phase burst pressure until animation/VFX
  feel can be judged manually.
- Improve early economy reachability through chest gold and shop-stock prices.

## Intentionally unchanged

- Tattoo element, shape and enchant-affix values remain unchanged in this pass.
  They already have dense 336-combo and status/enchant diagnostics, and changing
  them before manual combat feel would mix too many causes.
- Three-choice option values remain unchanged. Shop prices and chest gold were
  adjusted first so later playtests can reveal whether choice rewards still feel
  too generous.
- Merchant slot deterministic prices remain unchanged because diagnostics use
  them as stable routing evidence for merchant offer generation.
